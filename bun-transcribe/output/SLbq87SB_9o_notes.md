## Bible Study: Psalm 19 - "The World, the Word, and the Worshiper"
Divided into three sections:

### Speech of the World (vv. 1–6)
- Creation (heavens, sky, sun) declares God's glory and handiwork universally; no language barrier.
- Personifies nature as speaking knowledge of Creator; rational evidence against atheism (e.g., building implies builder).
- References Paul (Romans): creation reveals God's qualities, leaving man without excuse.
- Calls for childlike awe in creation.

### Supremacy of the Word (vv. 7–11)
- Describes God's Word (law, testimony, statutes, commandments, fear, judgments) as perfect, sure, right, pure, clean, true.
- Benefits: converts soul, wise simple, rejoices heart, enlightens eyes, warns, rewards.
- Invaluable (better than gold/honey); fear of Lord (awe/reverence) promotes purity/holiness.
- Anchor in chaotic world; sweet balm when meditated on.

### Sin of the Worshiper (vv. 12–14)
- Exposes human sin: unintentional ("secret faults" unaware of), willful ("presumptuous" deliberate).
- Prayer for cleansing, prevention of sin's dominion; freedom in Christ breaks sin's rule.
- Integrity: public words/private thoughts pleasing to God, who is strength/Redeemer.
- Call to repentance, faith in Christ's redemption.
