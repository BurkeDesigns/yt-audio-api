## Message: Biblical Masculinity in a Feminized World

**Core Thesis**: Sin corrupted God's design (Gen 1-3); world feminized due to weak/sinful men abdicating leadership, not strong women. Church must repent and reclaim obedience.

### 1. The Problem: Distorted Design (Gen 3)

- Eve usurped unassigned leadership; Adam passive/silent despite presence.
- Results: Blame-shifting (Adam blames Eve/God), ongoing conflict ("desire" for control in women, rule amid tension in men).
- Sin twists complementarity into rivalry; not from 1960s feminism, but Garden rebellion.

### 2. The Person: Christ (the Second Adam)

- Christ spoke truth (vs. Adam's silence), stepped forward (vs. withdrawal), sacrificed for bride (Eph 5:25).
- Obedience reverses Adam's disobedience (Rom 5:19); masculinity starts with repentance/faith, matures by the cross.
- Example: Man shifts from emotional absence to presence/prayer/leadership by emulating Christ.

### 3. The Practice: Obedience (1 Cor 16:13)

- **Be watchful**: Guard against temptation.
- **Stand firm**: Anchor in truth.
- **Act like men**: Take sacrificial responsibility; initiate toward hard things.
- **Be strong**: Endure quietly; lead homes spiritually, pursue holiness, confront sin.
- Call: Slay dragons (passivity, porn, cowardice, comfort); stop spectating—obey now.
